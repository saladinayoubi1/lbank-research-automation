# Backtest validity checklist

Every strategy-family study must record each item as `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE` with evidence. Any unresolved `FAIL` blocks Paper promotion.

- Point-in-time venue, symbol, contract, listing, delisting and universe definitions.
- Bybit primary provenance; Binance only corroborates; LBank remains tertiary/legacy research.
- Completed-bar signal timing and earliest executable observation.
- Missing, duplicate, reordered, open, stale and corrupt data behavior.
- Train/validation/test chronology and untouched final test set.
- Complete experiment/search count and multiple-testing treatment.
- Benchmark, hypothesis and falsification criteria registered before result inspection.
- Fees, spread, slippage, impact, queue position, funding, borrow, carry and transfer costs.
- Mark-price liquidation, margin, collateral, leverage and funding-sign reversal where relevant.
- Survivorship, look-ahead, label, preprocessing and feature leakage controls.
- Regime, start-date, delay, cost, parameter and venue sensitivity.
- Net return, drawdown, tail loss, turnover, exposure, capacity and window stability.
- Failed orders, partial fills, disconnections, stale quotes and hedge-leg failure.
- Reproducible source SHA, data digest, policy version, seed and environment.
- Explicit limitations, contradictory evidence, review date and obsolescence triggers.
- Research/Backtest/Paper boundary; no credentials, live orders or production authority.

Passing the checklist permits paper-forward observation only. It is not financial advice or evidence of future profitability.
