## Event-time algorithm and audit distinction

V113 reconstructs the full V111 research-only source-to-500-USDT capital ledger **once**, validates each original Wave-1/2 reference outcome once against its original **first two completed one-minute bars** (including the original Wave-2 right-confirmation at the first minute boundary), and streams the chronological, original V110 reservation/denial/known-release event journal. It never uses a later barrier-hit outcome to admit an earlier position. The standalone streaming function still relies on caller-supplied physical sources; only its **future-only** archival adapter checks the source through V111's original V103/V104 two-parser 42-page source audit and the V112 exact sealed-source overlay. Two offline parsers observing one archive are not independent exchange signatures.

Mandatory checkpoint coverage is exactly the original V112 policy: earliest fully closed source minute, every original six-coarse-event Wave-2 right-confirmation in physical source, its **two-minute completed candle reference cutoff**, all V110 original admission/denial or actually known later closure instants, and last physical source boundary. All times are minute-aligned, deduplicated and sorted. The new checkpoint builder **does not impose V112's 4,096 item cap and never samples or skips inconvenient checkpoints**. If original source completeness, timestamp order or cash journaling is compromised, the algorithm refuses a valid scientific proof rather than dropping the offending event.

Each mature reference screen is rederived from the physical source's two closed bars: original V107 necessary-pass/rejection, explicit bearish Spot abstention, valid original Wave-0/Wave-1 barriers, early source-end right-censoring, barrier touched during latency or reference bar, and second-minute **CLOSE** strictly inside the two landmarks. Reference screens and their source SHA fingerprints can never be rewritten by future price moves.

The independent capacity journal scan checks every hypothetical `BUY_SPOT` admission, original candidate identity, original decision and second-close reference-known instant, finite positive stress notional, original V110 **17bps each-side cost assumption**, cash including entry-side friction, maximum **250 USDT notional**, original per-reservation **2.5 USDT gapless stressed risk reserve**, maximum 2 active reservations, maximum 1 active per symbol, and aggregate shared cash **500 USDT** / stressed gapless risk **5 USDT**. All denial decisions remain in their original proposal count. A release can occur only for an active original hypothesis, at the time the first original physical postreference candle actually **closed after touching Wave-0 or Wave-1** (same-bar both assumed Wave-0 first). Unresolved or source-censored reservations retain cash until a separately observed legitimate release; no assumed profitable close, real fill or P/L.

The event-stream proof preserves the **exact V112 per-checkpoint prefix SHA256** from `json.dumps(event_prefix,sort_keys=True,ensure_ascii=False)` using an incremental SHA256 state and snapshot copy at each required cutoff, without materializing the entire growing event list afresh at every checkpoint. At each cutoff it also checks that the number of confirmed original coarse events, number of mature hypothetical references, original admitted/denied event count, cash reserved, risk reserved, and open hypothetical reservation count are source-chronologically consistent. It then reconciles the completed incremental ledger with the original full V110 V111 output exactly.

**Important methodology:** an incremental event-proof is **not** logically identical to recomputing the entire V111 source pipeline from scratch at each prefix. On small synthetic scenarios, V113 explicitly compares its checkpoint list, every count and every SHA256 journal prefix against V112's full differential replay. On larger future sources, it relies on the fixed, separately tested source/event invariants and performs one full original reconstruction, not thousands of full replays. The source itself must be independently verified before any future market assertion is possible.
