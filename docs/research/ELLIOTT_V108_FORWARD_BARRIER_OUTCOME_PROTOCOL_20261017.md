## Predeclared observation logic, NOT trade exits

Every original V107 candidate — `N-5` contiguous windows when `N>=6` confirmed coarse pivots — has separate V107 `necessary_pass` or `necessary_reject` status. V108 never revises these statuses; it only attaches a **separate postdecision outcome** referencing original candidate ordinal, decision time, the original six-event SHA and the predecision fine-event SHA. Two competing price barriers are sourced from the candidate's **Wave 0 origin CLOSE** and **Wave 1 endpoint CLOSE**. They are **price-path research landmarks**, not filled-order stop loss/take profit levels. A candidate lacking positive directional distance between these landmarks is `NONCOMPARABLE_GEOMETRY`, not a winner.

For each complete physical 1m OHLC bar beginning at the Wave-2 confirmation instant (with OHLC known only after the bar itself closes), observe the first barrier touch, stopping at the first touch or after 240 fully closed bars:
- `WAVE1_BARRIER_FIRST`: observed high (bull) or low (bear) reaches the Wave-1 endpoint before the origin is touched.
- `WAVE0_ORIGIN_FIRST`: observed low (bull) or high (bear) reaches the Wave-0 origin first.
- `SAME_MINUTE_BOTH_ASSUME_ORIGIN_FIRST`: both barriers are in the same minute's high-low span; **conservatively count as origin invalidation**, never infer high-before-low from 1-minute OHLC. This is not proof of the physical intraminute event order.
- `NO_BARRIER_BY_240_CLOSED_MINUTES`: neither observed during a full 240-minute horizon; **unresolved** rather than an implicit unsuccessful or profitable exit.
- `RIGHT_CENSORED_AFTER_SOURCE_END`: neither observed, but the protected historical source ends before the full 240 minutes elapse. A touched barrier **before source end** remains observable; otherwise do not label a missing future hour as a lost/won trade.
- `NONCOMPARABLE_GEOMETRY`: invalid directed Wave0/Wave1 barrier ordering; no fake price-path winner.

All six mutually exclusive categories must be recorded for *both* previously accepted and rejected candidate groups, including unresolved/censored/noncomparable cases. Original V107 candidate denominator is conserved exactly and refusal tallies are not silently replaced. An observed wave-1 barrier crossing does not establish a later five-wave Elliott pattern, independent ground truth, economic edge, actionable entry, true-positive rate, winner rate or P/L. No spread, slippage, fees, liquidity, latency, market impact, position holding/carrying or executable fills are calculated. No market entry or exit API exists.
