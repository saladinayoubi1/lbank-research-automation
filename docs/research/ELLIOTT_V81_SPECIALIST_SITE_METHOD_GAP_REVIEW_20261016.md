## Reviewed external references, and evidence level

2. EWI [Corrective Waves](https://www.elliottwave.com/waveopedia/corrective-waves/), [Zigzags](https://www.elliottwave.com/waveopedia/zigzags/), [Flats](https://www.elliottwave.com/waveopedia/flats/), [Combinations](https://www.elliottwave.com/waveopedia/combinations/): zigzag 5-3-5 vs flat 3-3-5 vs triangle 3-3-3-3-3; separately defined W-X-Y combinations. **Subwave witness required before naming a correction.**
3. EWI [Alternation](https://www.elliottwave.com/waveopedia/alternation/), [Equality](https://www.elliottwave.com/waveopedia/equality/), [Extension](https://www.elliottwave.com/waveopedia/extension/): wave-2/wave-4 alternation, Fibonacci/equality and extension are *soft probabilistic guidelines*, not exceptions to hard invalidation.
4. TradingView official [Elliott wave theory and tools](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000653212-elliott-wave-theory-and-tools/) and [Zigzag](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000591664-zigzag-indicator/): nested degree, provisional waves and explicit confirmation delay. **Chart labels can be retroactively placed at past pivot centers and must not be backdated as observable signals.**
5. TradingView third-party [Real Elliott Wave](https://www.tradingview.com/script/lLrD2OaV-Real-Elliott-Wave-MarketFragments/) and [Self-Validating Elliott Wave Engine](https://www.tradingview.com/script/U76W8mpk-Self-Validating-Elliott-Wave-Engine/): self-described top-down recursive parsing, competing hypothesis set, calibrated uncertainty and abstention. **Ideas only, not audited claims or code dependencies. No proprietary/open-source implementation copied.**
6. QuantConnect [Research Guide](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/writing-algorithms/key-concepts/research-guide), [Look-Ahead/Optimization](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/writing-algorithms/optimization/parameters): avoid post-selection/backtest look-ahead and evaluate held-out, point-in-time data. **Independent methodological guardrail, no proof Elliott works.**
