## Fixed simultaneous capacity assumptions

- One **shared 500 USDT cash-reference bankroll**, not 500 USDT per pair.
- At most **2 simultaneous hypothetical reservations**, at most **1 active reservation per symbol**, universe fixed at `BTCUSDT` and `ETHUSDT`, **BUY_SPOT only**. Borrowing, leverage, derivatives, futures and Spot short selling are not assumed.
- At most **250 USDT notional** per hypothetical admission, **2.5 USDT stressed gapless downside reserve per admitted candidate**, **5 USDT aggregate stressed gapless downside reserve**. The budget is a conservative *illustrative research policy*, not an empirically calibrated optimal risk allocation.
- Per-side **17 bps** cost stress consists of V109's *assumed* 10bps fee, 2bps half-spread and 5bps slippage. For each intent, nominal normalized gapless loss fraction is `(hypothetical_entry_close - confirmed_wave0_stop)/hypothetical_entry_close + 2*0.0017`. This fraction is used to cap the hypothetical notional. Entry-side friction is separately included in cash reserved: `notional * (1+0.0017)`, so **two 250-USDT gross reservations cannot spend more than the 500-USDT shared cash reference after costs**.
- Reservation sizing deterministically takes the minimum of per-position notional cap, available cash **including entry stress**, per-candidate stressed risk cap, and remaining aggregate stressed-risk capacity. Hypothetical USDT notional is **rounded down to 8 decimal places** to avoid crossing capacity due to rounding. The eight-decimal quantum is merely a numerical simulation convention; it does **NOT** represent actual Bybit lot size, quote precision or executable min-notional.
- These reserved risk numbers are **not a worst-case loss guarantee**: market gaps, insufficient liquidity, liquidation constraints and unmodeled actual fees could exceed the assumed Wave-0 stop loss. No P/L can be inferred from the cash-reservation ledger. No actual buy/sell order or real balance movement occurs.

## Strictly causal, deterministic event accounting

An immutable `Intent` contains only a research candidate ID, frozen Spot symbol and BUY_SPOT side, source decision timestamp, price reference-known timestamp, typed hypothetical already-known close price and previously confirmed Wave-0 reference stop. **It has no slot for post-decision P/L, V109 target-touch category, future actual close or pattern success**. Intent price and Wave-0 source must have been known at or before the original complete-minute decision timestamp. The pure calculator does not independently authenticate caller-supplied market source or verify that the caller included *all* V107 candidates; this must be a later explicit source-bound bridge and is not claimed in V110.

Every intent is accounted for, including denials; no silent dropping or score-based selection. Same-timestamp intents are ordered by fixed `symbol ASC, candidate ID ASC`, independent of profitability. A typed `Resolution` states an explicit, previously observable closed event and its **known-at** timestamp. A reservation is freed **only at the known-at timestamp**, never the earlier historical pivot center or the prior recorded close. At equal timestamps, already-known resolutions free capacity before new candidate intents. Late-known results cannot revise previous allocation decisions. A candidate that was denied cannot fabricate a release to mint cash capacity. Duplicate candidates or releases, future data, unknown symbols, shorts, NaN, wrong-side stop, misaligned source timestamps and unknown release reasons fail closed.

Results contain an **append-only chronological hypothetical admission / denial / known-release event ledger**, full original proposal denominator, cash-reserved and remaining shared cash, aggregate stressed gapless risk and current open synthetic reservations. Historic event rows remain byte-identical when later outcomes are appended; a current summary naturally evolves when events happen. All output unconditionally reports `trade_count=0`, `entry_signal=false`, `paper_eligible=false`, `live_enabled=false` and `actual_net_pnl=null`.

## Adversarial tests and interpretation

Synthetic tests cover BTC/ETH sharing the bankroll and risk pool; same-symbol overlap; deterministic simultaneous prioritization; absent/late-known exit releases; genuine same-minute releases; twenty overlapping synthetic candidates (all remain in denominator); two close-stop near-250 USDT candidates where entry friction would otherwise overspend the 500 USDT bankroll; round-down capital funding; release of notional **plus its buy-side cash stress**; wrong-way Wave-0 reference; attempts at short, extra symbol, forged outcome field, future price known after decision, future or duplicated release, unknown candidate, and immutable parent/manifest evidence.

V110 does NOT itself replay actual Bybit historical data, choose candidate trading signals, assign economic fills, handle open-portfolio mark-to-market, measure opportunity cost, apply symbol correlation, account for execution lot-size constraints or demonstrate risk-adjusted returns. It is an isolated fail-closed capacity primitive intended for a future **source-bound research-only** bridge after the protected market period, requiring all V103/V104 source audits and original V107/V108/V109 causal attribution.
