## Causal enumeration invariants

- Use original source-derived, strictly increasing time and confirmation order for two independent V99 close-only scales (fine=3, coarse=8). V100 validates physical source-minute indexing and the original, complete event streams. Require six **consecutive** coarse events per candidate; fewer than six yields *zero eligible candidates*, not an invented Wave-2 hypothesis.
- If the source contains `N` confirmed coarse events, the **exact denominator** is `max(0,N-5)`, including rejected candidates. The first five coarse events are context-only for enumeration. Do not cherry-pick the best-looking candidate, merge adjacent overlapping windows, drop inconvenient fine pivots, or remove candidates that conflict with later price action.
- Each window's as-of is `coarse[i+5].confirmed_ms`, not `coarse[i+5].center_ms`. Use a monotone right-bound cutoff to select `fine[j].confirmed_ms <= as-of` and pass precisely that prefix to frozen V106. All rejection codes from V106 are stored, with both **mutually exclusive first-rejection tallies** (summing to rejected windows) and *overlapping* all-reasons counts. Passes + rejections **must equal the exact denominator**.
- The serialized candidate ledger records candidate ordinal, original index range, original decision UTC milliseconds, number and SHA digest of fine events actually known at that moment, the six original coarse digest, necessary Wave 1–2 skeleton pass/rejection, and explicit no-order/no-elliott-certification flags. The ledger **must never record the number or identity of later-confirmed events**, even as a non-trading metadata field: those are not known at the earlier decision time.
- A synthetic prefix extension test verifies **byte-equivalent candidate ledger entries** when new coarse/fine events become confirmed later. A bug initially included `fine_confirmed_later_not_allowed` in historical records, which changed with future data despite fixed decisions; this was identified by the adversarial test and **removed before release**.
- A passing necessary Wave 1–2 contour remains only a **partial geometric hypothesis**, not a verified Elliott pattern, real order, profitable trade, prediction precision, or measured false-positive rate. The census may eventually calculate empirical pass/reject rates but those rates are NOT prediction success rates; no actual market truth labels or post-entry returns are available now. This intentionally produces no P/L, broker or Demo orders.
