## Source-time replay, no postdecision selection

- The auditor takes a **complete original physical 1m source**, plus confirmed coarse/fine V99 event streams and an independently reconstructed **full** V111 report. The standalone function only evaluates *caller-provided streams*, not provider-authenticity; its later future-only adapter requires V111's V103/V104 two-parser complete source gate and the original SHA-signed local bytes before constructing the stream itself. Those two parsers inspect the same archive, not two separate Bybit-signed market observations.
- Mandatory checkpoints are the original first completed source minute, **every right-confirmed Wave-2 candidate cutoff**, its first safely available two-minute closed reference cutoff, **every actual V110 synthetic admission, denial or known-closed barrier event**, and the final source cutoff. Cutoffs are sorted, deduplicated, aligned to complete minute closes. **No sampling**, random omission, or favorable-subset selection. If over **4,096 mandatory checkpoints**, auditor **fails closed** rather than claiming exhaustiveness; a later scalable implementation must separately preserve every mandatory checkpoint.
- At EACH checkpoint `t`, rebuild the source from exactly the 1m OHLC bars fully closed by `t`; discard all coarse and fine extrema with confirmation time *after* `t`. Recreate the original V107 complete six-event candidate enumeration, V111 two-closed-minute reference conditions, and V110 shared-account chronological hypothetical reservation/release ledger. No earlier than the original Wave-2 right-confirmation; source price reference is the already-closed second minute CLOSE, not its unfilled historical OPEN.
- The resulting full-prefix V110 **event ledger** must exactly match the subset of final V110 event rows whose original `known_at_ms <= t`. Every old candidate ID and original admission status in that prefix must also match its status in the full report. Reference abstention/pass decisions may be provisional for the first two minutes after original right-confirmation; once the second postdecision minute closes, its **entire original reference-screen record** is immutable and must exactly match the mature full report. Future Wave-3/4/5 price moves cannot change old capacity decisions.
- Every historical source prefix must conserve the original shared **500 USDT** cash reservoir, reserve future entry-side 17bps friction when funding an imaginary BUY_SPOT, and retain within the frozen V110 max **5 USDT stress-risk reserve**. Missing/late-known hypothetical exit does NOT free future cash in the past. Original rejected and bearish candidates remain in all candidate counts and cannot be retroactively promoted to Spot short.
- This is not a trading simulator or economic portfolio reconciliation. V110 gapless synthetic risk allowances are not guaranteed stop-loss limits; there are no real Bybit fills, account-specific fees, minimum order sizes, adverse impact models, dividends, actual P/L, liquidity or profit proofs.
