## Deduplication and metadata policy

The six records are keyed by DOI and title/author/year checks. Promotional/practitioner pages are excluded from the core pack. `Zarrabi et al. (2017)` is normalized to DOI `10.1016/j.irfa.2016.12.010`; `Ulkü & Prodan (2013)` is normalized to DOI `10.1016/j.irfa.2013.08.005`. The same records are exported as BibTeX, RIS and CSL JSON in this directory.

## Evidence-to-experiment boundary

This pack supports a falsifiable EMA/trend-following experiment, not a profitability claim. Any NEXUS experiment using it must predeclare signal timing, next-observation execution, realistic spread/commission/slippage/turnover assumptions, chronological out-of-sample or walk-forward evaluation, parameter-family disclosure, robustness across regimes/symbols/timeframes, benchmark comparison, statistical uncertainty, invalidation criteria and a separate paper-forward gate. Historical success alone cannot authorize live trading.
