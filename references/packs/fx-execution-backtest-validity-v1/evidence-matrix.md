## Package decision

Included in the core library because each item has verifiable authorship, stable identity, explicit methodology and direct applicability. No promotional or anonymous sources were admitted. The package establishes two mandatory NEXUS controls:

1. Execution models must be venue-aware and include spread, fees, market impact, latency, funding/roll, rejection/last-look and settlement assumptions where applicable.
2. Backtest claims must disclose the full model search, use leakage-safe holdouts, and apply at least one multiplicity-aware procedure in addition to cost and regime sensitivity tests.
