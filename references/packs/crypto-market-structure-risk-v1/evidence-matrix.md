# Crypto Market Structure and Risk Evidence Pack v1.0.0

Review date: 2026-08-05
Scope: research and paper-trading only. No personalized advice or real-order execution.

## Inclusion policy
Core entries require verifiable publisher metadata and at least one of: official standard/report, peer-reviewed publication, reproducible academic study, or documented market/operational evidence. Promotional, anonymous, duplicate, unverifiable, and metadata-poor material is excluded.

## Required NEXUS gates
1. Point-in-time universe, delistings and venue history.
2. Bid/ask, taker/maker fees, impact, latency, funding, borrow, transfers and failed withdrawals.
3. Walk-forward evaluation with purging/embargo where labels overlap.
4. Full strategy-search disclosure and multiplicity-aware inference.
5. Exchange/custodian default, stablecoin depeg, oracle, bridge and liquidation stress tests.
6. No claim of deployable alpha without independent post-publication evidence and net-cost replication.

## Excluded from core
Unverified social posts, exchange marketing, influencer content, anonymous strategy reports, backtests without point-in-time data, and duplicated working-paper/published versions where the final peer-reviewed version is available.
